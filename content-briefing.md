# Content Briefing: Kev & Andre Peter's Wedding Website

This is the working source of truth for the site. It started from Wedding_Website_Content_Briefing.md (November 9, 2026, the two celebration version) and then folds in every decision made since in conversation with Andre Peter. Where the two disagree, the later decision wins, and the list at the end ("Where this overrides the November 9 briefing") says exactly where.

## The structure in one paragraph

The wedding is two linked celebrations one week apart. The **Nigerian Ceremony** (church wedding and Urhobo traditional ceremony, same day) is on **June 12, 2027 in Lagos, Nigeria**. The **Brazilian Reception**, a smaller gathering at a beach house, is on **June 19, 2027 in Salvador, Bahia**. Espaco Tropicalia is no longer the venue. The site reads as one love story told in two homes, with God at the center, and every guest treated as part of the story.

## Names and tone

- Andre Peter goes by "Peter" to Brazilian family and friends and "Andre" to Nigerian family and friends, so the site always shows "Andre Peter".
- Her name: "Oghenekevwe" only in page `<title>` tags and on the Home page, "Kev" everywhere else in the body, the footer and calendar entries.
- Hashtag: #OurAlliance2027 (a play on "aliança", the Portuguese word for wedding ring and covenant). Confirm with Kev before it goes on signage.
- Tone: elegant and timeless, warm and intimate. Bilingual, English and Portuguese as full parallel languages, Urhobo and Pidgin phrases kept as they are in both.
- Writing style: no em dashes, en dashes or hyphens used as punctuation, in the site, in chat replies and in these notes. The one exception is a citation attribution, such as the Bible verse reference. Established terms such as "aso-ebi" and given names are left alone.

## Site map

1. **Home.** A sunrise that builds as the visitor scrolls (a fixed sky behind the whole page with the content in front of it, lavender and rose dawn warming to gold, a sun rising over the sea, finishing as the footer arrives, static under reduced motion), both dates and cities ("Lagos. June 12." and "Salvador. June 19."), a countdown to the nearer date with a note about the reception a week later, the line "It is important to us that God is at the center of our wedding", the two event cards with add to calendar links, the hashtag with share buttons.
2. **Our Story.** How they met, the journey, a favorite memory, and a painting below the text. The long proposal story (July 9, 2026, Porto da Barra) was removed from the page in November 2026 at Andre Peter's request.
3. **Our Alliance.** One page: the couple (two full portraits), the wedding party, the family, and a tribute to Kev's father.
4. **Our Faith.** Both faith stories, the verse, the ceremony songs, a welcome for guests of every background.
5. **Our Ceremonies** (navigation group): **Nigerian Ceremony** and **Brazilian Reception**.
6. **Travel and Accommodation** (navigation group): **Getting to Lagos** and **Getting to Salvador**.
7. **RSVP.** Event selector (Nigerian Ceremony, Brazilian Reception, both, or cannot come), number of guests, dietary notes, a travel or visa help flag.
8. **Registry.** Honeymoon or cash fund. Pix key live, an international option pending.
9. **Gallery.** One shared album per city.
10. **FAQ.** Answered per city where it matters.
11. **Contact.** A form placeholder, no personal contact details published.

Retired pages and where their content went: Meet the Couple and Wedding Party and Family became Our Alliance. Traditions and Menu moved into the Nigerian Ceremony (traditions, glossary, Nigerian dishes) and the Brazilian Reception (Brazilian dishes). Schedule moved into the Brazilian Reception as a draft order of the day. Event Details became the Brazilian Reception. Travel became Getting to Salvador. Old addresses redirect through vercel.json.

A persistent navigation with the English and Portuguese toggle sits on every page, and a footer of small flowers in varying sizes sits in the bottom right corner of every page.
