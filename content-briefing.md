| Phrase | How it sounds | Origin | Meaning and usage |
|---|---|---|---|
| Migwo | mee gwoh | Urhobo | "I am kneeling," the ultimate sign of respect when greeting Urhobo elders. |
| Vrendo | vren doh | Urhobo | "Good morning" or "Welcome," how an elder responds to Migwo. |
| Item 7 | | Nigerian slang | The food and refreshment segment of an event program. |
